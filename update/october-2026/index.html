2 · Shipped

Pins Studio Released

From a pin, the traveller picks a layout, places photos, adds stickers and text, and exports a story or carousel to the share sheet. Separate from the older single-image place-story share.

Why I built this

A pin was a private record. The only export was one place-story image — so people still left the app to make an Instagram post, and the pin’s place, date, and companions did not come with them. Studio is the composer on the pin they already dropped: Instagram-native sizes, on the phone, no photo upload.
