Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

In Plain Words

Travelpin is built around your journeys — and your memories.

We do not sell your data.
We do not run ads.
We do not track you across apps.

We only collect what is needed to make Travelpin work — and nothing more.

If you choose to add personal details (like saved places, trips, or notes), they are stored securely and used only to power your experience.

Our Approach to Privacy

Most travel apps treat your data like a resource. We treat it like something personal — because it is.

Your trips, your places, your memories — they belong to you.

What We Collect (and Why)

1. Account Information (Optional)

If you create an account:

  • Name
  • Email

Used for:

  • Sync across devices
  • Backup and recovery

2. Trips and Flight Data

When you add trips:

  • Flight number, route, timings
  • Airports and travel history

Used for:

  • Flight tracking
  • ETA calculations
  • Trip organization

3. Location (Only With Permission)

If you enable location, we may use:

  • Your current location (for ETA)
  • Saved locations (home, office, etc.)
  • Airport proximity (to improve travel insights)

Important:

  • We do not track you continuously
  • We only use location when needed for features
  • Background tracking is minimized

4. Pins and Memories

When you use Pins:

  • Places you save
  • Notes you write
  • Tags (for example: beach, romantic, late night)
  • Visit dates

Used for:

  • Building your personal map
  • Memory recall
  • AI suggestions (optional features)

5. Basecamp Insights

We may process:

  • Travel patterns
  • Routes
  • Time-of-day preferences

Used to:

  • Show your travel insights
  • Help you understand your journeys

6. Device and Technical Data

We collect minimal technical data:

  • Device type
  • App version
  • Crash logs

Used to:

  • Improve reliability
  • Fix issues

AI Features

Travelpin uses AI to:

  • Suggest checklists
  • Generate insights
  • Enhance trip planning

Design principle:

  • AI uses only the data needed
  • We avoid storing unnecessary personal content
  • Your raw notes and memories are not used for training external models

Analytics (Minimal and Anonymous)

We collect anonymous usage data like:

  • Feature usage
  • Screen interactions

Used to:

  • Improve the app
  • Understand what users value

We do NOT:

  • Track you across apps
  • Build advertising profiles

Ads

Travelpin does not use ads. We do not collect or share data for advertising.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data only to:

  • Provide core features (Trips, Pins, ETA)
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Improve product experience
  • Offer support

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data.

We only share data when required to operate the service, such as:

  • Cloud infrastructure providers
  • Flight and mapping APIs

These partners only receive the minimum data necessary.

Security

We take security seriously:

  • Encrypted communication (HTTPS)
  • Secure storage
  • Access controls

Your Control

You can:

  • Edit or delete your data
  • Disable location anytime
  • Request account deletion

Contact: hello@travelpinapp.com

Children

Travelpin is not intended for users under 13.

International Use

Your data may be processed on servers outside your country, depending on infrastructure.

Changes

We may update this policy as Travelpin evolves. If changes are significant, we will notify you.
