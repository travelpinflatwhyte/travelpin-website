Questions

Subscriptions are purchased inside the Travelpin iOS app.

How do I subscribe?
Download Travelpin, create an account, and tap Upgrade to Pro in Settings — or save a 6th owned trip to see the paywall. Payment is handled securely by Apple.
What is the Week Pass?
A one-time purchase that gives you 7 days of Pro. It does not auto-renew. Ideal for a single busy travel week.
Is there a free trial on Annual?
New subscribers may be eligible for a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Apple charges you when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Eligibility is determined by Apple.
How do I cancel or get a refund?
Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple according to their policies.
What happens if I downgrade?
You keep all trips you already created. On the free tier you can own up to 5 non-archived trips — you won't be able to create new ones until you're under the limit or upgrade again.
New phone — will Pro transfer?
Yes. Sign in with the same account and tap Restore Purchases on the paywall. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. The week pass is a one-time purchase and does not auto-renew. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your data.
